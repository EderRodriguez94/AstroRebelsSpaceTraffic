# Asset acceptance checklist

- [ ] Original design; no direct copying or unlicensed source material.
- [ ] License/provenance recorded for every source and contribution.
- [ ] Ship, passenger, dock and faction symbols remain readable at mobile scale.
- [ ] Contrast and non-color cues pass accessibility review.
- [ ] Pivot, logical-cell scale, orientation and material are documented.
- [ ] Reference-device LOD, polygon and texture budgets are recorded.
- [ ] Asset does not introduce ambiguity into puzzle reading.
- [ ] Product owner approves representative direction before production use.
