# Astro Rebels Traffic — Original Art Bible

## Direction

Original cartoon sci-fi: clear silhouettes, bold functional shapes, restrained surface detail and faction symbols that remain legible at portrait mobile scale. Direct copying of existing games, films, logos, characters or recognizable trademarked designs is prohibited.

## Readability

Ships, passengers and docks must remain distinguishable at the smallest supported reference viewport. Color is never the only cue; every faction uses a unique symbol and accessible label. Maintain strong foreground/background contrast and test both light and dark scenes.

## Construction

Use documented pivots, consistent logical-cell scale, readable materials and stable orientation cues. LOD, polygon and texture budgets are selected after reference-device profiling and recorded with each asset review.
